About WorldView

A faster way to scan world news

WorldView collects recent reports from trusted sources, organizes them by topic and continent, and groups related coverage so you can move from headline to original source quickly.

Trusted sources Collects reports from established global media and public institutions.
Regional queues Keeps separate news queues for each continent, with Global sorted by heat.
Heat ranking Stories are ranked by freshness, source weight and coverage signals.
Original reports Open the source article and compare related coverage when needed.
